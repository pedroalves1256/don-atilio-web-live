(07) FAQ

Before yourfirst class

I have never boxed. Can I join?

Yes. Downtown Boxing Club is for all skill levels. Group classes start with a warm-up and build up to technique, so beginners fit right in.

What ages is the Young Boxer Program for?

Kids 8 to 15. Classes are Monday to Thursday at 4 p.m. and focus on discipline, focus and confidence.

Can I pause or cancel my membership?

Yes. Memberships auto-renew every 4 weeks and you can pause or cancel anytime.

When is sparring?

Sparring is on Fridays at 6 p.m. Talk to the coaches first so they can match you with the right partner.

Do you take Wellhub?

Yes, Downtown Boxing Club is a Wellhub partner. Check your plan in the Wellhub app.
