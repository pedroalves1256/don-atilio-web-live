(05) FAQ

Before yourfirst class

I have never boxed. Can I join?

Absolutely. Downtown Boxing Club is a gym for everyone. Coaches adapt every class to your level, from your very first day.

Do I have to spar?

No. Many members train only for fitness. Sparring is always optional.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 6:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m. and 4:30–7:00 p.m., Saturday 6:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m. Call ahead to confirm class times.

How much is a membership?

Call or text (850) 257-5783 and we will share current rates and options. Wellhub members are welcome too.

Is there parking?

Yes, there is parking at the gym, plus changing rooms inside.

Your firstround startshere.

Send us a text and we will reply with class times, rates and everything you need to get started.
